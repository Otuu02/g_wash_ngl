COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about pricing and booking? We've got clear answers.

You can click the "Book a Service" button anywhere on this page, choose your desired service, enter your phone number and address in Lagos, Abuja, or Port Harcourt, and tap submit. A certified pro will be dispatched to your location!

Pricing on G-Wash is clear, affordable, and upfront. Every service has transparent standard rates with zero hidden charges. Payment is secured safely by Paystack escrow and only released when the job is completed to your satisfaction.

When a provider is matched to your job, the app generates a unique 4-digit OTP code on your screen. When the provider physically arrives at your gate or doorstep, you give them the code so they can verify their identity and commence the job safely.

We process payments securely via Paystack. You can pay using Nigerian Debit/Credit Cards (Mastercard, Visa, Verve), Bank USSD transfer, or top up your in-app G-Wash Wallet for one-tap instant bookings.
